Mechanism

These controls show two independent motions. The cover carries the beam roots; inertia makes the tip masses lag. Motion is an illustration, not a time-domain simulation.

Component details

Purple: piezoelectric beams. Gold: return springs. Copper: proof masses. Dark frame: stationary housing.

The six structural stops carry foot loads after the 2 mm stroke.

Dimensionally defined concept. STEP uses millimetres. The 200 mm dimension is flat-to-flat; opposite corners are 230.94 mm apart. Load capacity, weather sealing, fatigue and energy output have not been validated. Electronic parts and wire routes are reference envelopes.
